\# XR Fundamentals



\## 1. Introduction



Extended Reality (XR) is a broad term used for immersive technologies that combine the physical and digital worlds. The three major areas of XR are Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), and Mixed Reality (MR).



\## 2. Augmented Reality (AR)



Augmented Reality adds digital information or virtual objects to the user's view of the real environment.



\### Examples



\- AR navigation

\- Educational AR applications

\- Object recognition

\- Retail product visualization

\- Mobile AR applications



\### Key Characteristics



\- Real-world environment remains visible.

\- Digital content is overlaid on the physical environment.

\- Usually uses cameras, sensors, and tracking technologies.



\## 3. Virtual Reality (VR)



Virtual Reality creates a computer-generated environment in which the user can experience an immersive digital world.



\### Examples



\- VR games

\- Training simulators

\- Virtual laboratories

\- Virtual tours

\- Medical training



\### Key Characteristics



\- Provides an immersive digital environment.

\- Commonly uses a VR headset.

\- Allows users to interact with virtual objects and environments.



\## 4. Mixed Reality (MR)



Mixed Reality combines physical and digital environments and allows digital objects to interact with the real world.



For example, a virtual object may appear on a real table while remaining spatially aware of the table's position.



\## 5. Comparison



| Feature | AR | VR | MR |

|---|---|---|---|

| Real world visible | Yes | Usually no | Yes |

| Virtual environment | Partial | Complete | Combined |

| Immersion | Low–Medium | High | High |

| Typical device | Smartphone / AR glasses | VR headset | MR headset |

| Interaction with physical environment | Limited | Limited | Strong |



\## 6. Applications of XR



XR technologies are increasingly used in:



\- Education

\- Healthcare

\- Engineering

\- Gaming

\- Architecture

\- Industrial training

\- Entertainment

\- Retail



\## 7. Conclusion



XR combines different immersive technologies to create new ways of interacting with digital information. AR enhances the real world, VR creates fully virtual environments, and MR combines physical and digital environments. Understanding these technologies provides a foundation for developing immersive applications.
